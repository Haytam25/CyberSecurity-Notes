## 🛡️ Patch Management

### 🎯 What is Patch Management?

> The process of identifying, acquiring, testing, and applying updates (patches) to software and systems to correct vulnerabilities.

---

### 🔁 Patch Management Lifecycle

1. **Identification** – Find out what needs to be patched (based on scan or vendor advisory).
    
2. **Evaluation** – Risk assessment and testing the patch in a staging environment.
    
3. **Deployment** – Rolling out patches to production systems.
    
4. **Verification** – Ensure patches were applied correctly.
    
5. **Documentation** – Record what was patched and when.
    

---

### 📦 Types of Patches

- 🔐 **Security Patch** – Fixes specific vulnerabilities.
    
- 🧩 **Feature Update** – Adds functionality.
    
- ⚠️ **Hotfix** – Urgent fix outside of regular patch schedule.
    
- 📦 **Service Pack** – Collection of updates and fixes.
    

---

### 🧪 Best Practices

- Test patches before full deployment.
    
- Have a rollback plan.
    
- Apply critical security patches ASAP.
    
- Use automation tools (e.g., **WSUS**, **SCCM**, **Ansible**) for deployment.
    

---

## 💡 Summary

- Vulnerability scanning is **proactive** and **automated**.
    
- Use **credentialed scans** for accuracy, **non-credentialed** for outsider view.
    
- Patch management is critical to reduce risk exposure.
    
- Always **test patches**, **deploy smartly**, and **track what you change**.
