# 802.11 Wireless Standards

## Overview
802.11 standards define how wireless LANs operate. Each version provides different speed, frequency, and bandwidth capabilities.

## Common Standards

| Standard | Frequency | Max Speed | Max Range | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| 802.11a  | 5 GHz     | 54 Mbps   | Short     | Less interference, less range |
| 802.11b  | 2.4 GHz   | 11 Mbps   | Long      | Prone to interference |
| 802.11g  | 2.4 GHz   | 54 Mbps   | Long      | Backward compatible with 802.11b |
| 802.11n  | 2.4/5 GHz | 600 Mbps  | Longer    | MIMO support |
| 802.11ac | 5 GHz     | ~1 Gbps+  | Medium    | High throughput, multiple channels |
| 802.11ax | 2.4/5 GHz | ~10 Gbps  | High      | Known as Wi-Fi 6, improved efficiency |

## Concepts
- **MIMO**: Multiple Input Multiple Output – allows multiple data streams.
- **Channel Bonding**: Combines multiple channels for higher throughput.
- **MU-MIMO**: Multi-User MIMO – simultaneous connections.
