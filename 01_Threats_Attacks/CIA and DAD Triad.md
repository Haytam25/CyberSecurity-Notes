# CIA
Before we can describe something as _secure_, we need to consider better what makes up security. When you want to judge the security of a system, you need to think in terms of the security triad: 
## Confidentiality
- Prevents unauthorized access to data.
- Methods: Encryption, access control, classification, need-to-know.

## Integrity
- Ensures data is unaltered and trustworthy.
- Methods: Hashing, digital signatures, checksums.

## Availability
- Ensures systems/data are accessible when needed.
- Methods: Redundancy, backups, failover systems, DoS protection.

# DAD
The security of a system is attacked through one of several means. It can be via the disclosure of secret data, alteration of data, or destruction of data.
## Disclosure
	Is the opposite of confidentiality. In other words, disclosure of confidential data would be an attack on confidentiality.
## Alteration
	is the opposite of Integrity. For example, the integrity of a cheque is indispensable.
## Destruction/Denial 
	 Is the opposite of Availability.
