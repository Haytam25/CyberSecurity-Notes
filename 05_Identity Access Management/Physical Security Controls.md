## 1. Deterrent Controls
- Signs, warning labels
- Lighting
- Guards

## 2. Preventive Controls
- Fences, gates, locks, access badges
- Turnstiles, mantraps
- Biometric scanners

## 3. Detective Controls
- CCTV (security cameras)
- Motion detectors
- Intrusion alarms

## 4. Environmental Controls
- HVAC (temperature and humidity control)
- Fire suppression (water, gas, FM-200, CO₂)
- EMI shielding
- Hot aisle / Cold aisle layouts
