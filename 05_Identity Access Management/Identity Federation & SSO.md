## **Single Sign-On (SSO)**
- Login once → access to multiple systems.
- **Improves user experience**, reduces password fatigue.

## **Federation**
- Identity shared across **multiple systems/organizations**.
- Example: Login with Google or Microsoft account.
## 🧠 5. Key Concepts & Best Practices

- **Least Privilege**: Only the access necessary to perform tasks.
- **Separation of Duties**: Split roles to prevent abuse/fraud.
- **Implicit Deny**: Block everything unless **explicitly allowed**.
- **Access Control List (ACL)**: List of permissions tied to a resource.
- **Privilege Creep**: Unchecked growth of permissions over time → needs periodic reviews.
