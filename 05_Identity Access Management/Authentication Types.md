## Authentication Factors

| Factor Type            | Example                      |
|------------------------|------------------------------|
| Something you know     | Password, PIN                |
| Something you have     | Smartcard, OTP token         |
| Something you are      | Fingerprint, face scan       |
| Somewhere you are      | GPS location, IP address     |
| Something you do       | Typing pattern, behavior     |

## Authentication Methods
### a. **Single-Factor Authentication (SFA)**
- One method (usually password).
- **Weak security**.

### b. **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**
- Two or more of the following:
  - 🔤 Something you **know** (password, PIN)
  - 🔐 Something you **have** (token, phone, smartcard)
  - 🧬 Something you **are** (biometrics)

### c. **Biometric Authentication**
- Examples: fingerprint, iris scan, face recognition.
- Highly secure but raises **privacy** concerns.
## Other Concepts
- TOTP (Time-based One-Time Password)
- HOTP (HMAC-based One-Time Password)
- Biometrics:
  - Accurate and hard to spoof
  - Raises privacy concerns
