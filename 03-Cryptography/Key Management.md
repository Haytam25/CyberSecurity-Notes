## 🔑 Key Management

## **Key Exchange Algorithms**:

- **Diffie-Hellman (DH)** – Securely exchange keys over insecure channel
    
- **ECDHE** – Uses Elliptic Curve (faster/smaller)
    

## **Key Escrow**:

- 3rd party stores backup copies of encryption keys (for recovery or compliance)
    

## **Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS)**:

- Uses ephemeral keys that are not reused
    
- Prevents decryption of past sessions if private key is leaked
