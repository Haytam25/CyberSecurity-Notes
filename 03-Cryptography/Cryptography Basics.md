## Cryptography Fundamentals

### **Goals of Cryptography**:

- **Confidentiality** – Ensure data is only accessible by authorized users
    
- **Integrity** – Ensure data has not been altered
    
- **Authentication** – Verify identity
    
- **Non-repudiation** – Prove the sender cannot deny sending the data
    

### **Encryption vs Hashing vs Encoding**:

|Technique|Purpose|Reversible|Example|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Encryption**|Protect confidentiality|✅|AES, RSA|
|**Hashing**|Verify integrity|❌|SHA-256|
|**Encoding**|Format for transport|✅|Base64|
