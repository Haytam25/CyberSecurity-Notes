## 🔐 Public Key Infrastructure (PKI)

- Framework for managing public-key encryption
    
- Involves **Certificate Authorities (CA)** to issue and verify certificates
    
- **Certificates** bind public keys to identities
    

## **Key Concepts**:

- Certificate Signing Request (CSR)
    
- Root CA, Intermediate CA
    
- Certificate Revocation List (CRL) / OCSP
