#### 🔍 Other Crypto Topics

- **Salting Passwords**: Random data added before hashing → prevents rainbow table attacks
    
- **PGP/GPG**: Email encryption and digital signing using asymmetric encryption
    
- **One-Time Pad (OTP)**: Unbreakable encryption if pad is random, never reused
    
- **Tokens**: Used for MFA; may generate OTP codes
